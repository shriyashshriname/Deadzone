# DEADZONE Project Context

## Identity
DEADZONE: Last Stand. Grounded top-down/isometric action shooter in a biological-outbreak setting (research facilities, military sites, industrial areas). Not a MOBA clone, not sci-fi.
Hook: EVERY SHOT IS A DECISION. Gunfire makes Noise, Noise draws the infected, so fighting always has a cost.
Long-term: AI infected, bosses, tactical roles, loot, objectives, 4-player co-op, then PvPvE.

## Confirmed by the founder
Unity 6 (C#, URP). Android and iOS. Top-down/isometric camera. Both shooting modes (push-to-fire, and aim + fire button). Hybrid weapon acquisition. Friendly fire off. Single-player first, multiplayer later. Ads + cosmetic IAP (skins, gems), no pay-to-win. No extra paid services.

## Proposed (defaults; the founder can override)
- Single-player = repeatable extraction runs of 6-8 minutes: deploy, scavenge, fight, boss vault with a 60-second loot window, extract.
- First slice: one research-facility map, one character, three weapons (AR, shotgun, SMG), three enemies (Crawler, Brute, Spitter), one boss (Crawler King), pickups, extraction, local save.
- Hit-location multipliers and weapon-weight penalties off in v1. Rarity tiers 1-3 only in v1.
- Downed state with a 5 s revive; no unlimited respawns.
- Later content: Hunter, Screamer, Sniper, DMR, LMG, more classes (Medic, Engineer, Scout), 3 bosses (Crawler King, Brute, The Experiment).

## Founder constraints
Solo human founder. Windows PC (Ryzen 5 7600X, RTX 4060 Ti, 16 GB RAM, 1 TB SSD). VS Code + Antigravity (agy) terminal. Gemini 3.8 Flash through the Jio AI Pro offer. No extra subscriptions or spending without approval.

## Current objective
Gate 0, then a graybox combat sandbox on a real Android phone.
