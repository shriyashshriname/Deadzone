# Publishing, Budget and Platform Constraints
Research date 16 Nov 2026. Fees and policies change; re-check official pages before any release.

## No-extra-cost policy
No paid AI API or subscription, no paid assets, backend, cloud build or Mac rental, no store fee until release-ready, all without founder approval.

## Unity Personal
Free for eligible users under US$200,000 revenue/funding in the last 12 months. Re-check if the business grows. (unity.com/products, unity.com/pages/license-compliance)

## Google Play
- One-time US$25 registration.
- New personal accounts (created after 13 Nov 2023): closed test with at least 12 testers opted in for 14 continuous days, then apply for production access.
- Needs a privacy policy, Data Safety form, ads declaration if the app has ads, content rating.
(support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/6112435, /14151465, /9859455, /10144311)

## Apple
- Developer Program US$99/year. Unity generates an Xcode project; local Xcode build and signing need macOS. A Windows PC alone cannot finish an iOS build.
- Zero-cost path: develop on Windows, keep iOS compatibility, use a borrowed or lab Mac for the final build, pay only when release-ready.
- Digital goods and in-game currency generally need Apple In-App Purchase. Paid randomized rewards need odds disclosure. (developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/)

## Ads and IAP
No ad SDK until the game is fun without ads, SDK terms and data collection are reviewed, privacy disclosures are ready and performance impact is measured.

## Antigravity and the Jio offer
Use the model actually shown in your agy model selector. Official Jio FAQ: the promotional offer is closed to new claimers; users who already claimed it continue under the qualifying-plan terms. Features such as /swarm, /teamwork and extra models can depend on the plan, so test them. (antigravity.google/docs/models, antigravity.google/docs/subagents, jio.com/help/faq/mobile/offers/google-gemini-offer/)

## Minimum unavoidable public-launch costs
Android US$25 once. iOS US$99/year, plus Mac access if none is available. Servers, backend or analytics only if chosen later. None is needed to start building.

## Free-marketing ideas
Gameplay clips, devlogs, indie communities, tester groups, an itch.io page for early feedback.
